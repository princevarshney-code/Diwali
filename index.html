शुभ दीपावली Happy Deepawali

May the light of every diya bring peace to your heart, prosperity to your home, and happiness to your life.

Mantras for the night

Gayatri Mantra

ॐ भूर्भुवः स्वः तत्सवितुर्वरेण्यं भर्गो देवस्य धीमहि।
धियो यो नः प्रचोदयात्॥

We meditate upon the divine light of the Supreme; may it illuminate and inspire our intellect.

Lakshmi prayer

ॐ श्रीं महालक्ष्म्यै नमः॥

Salutations to Goddess Mahalakshmi, the goddess associated with prosperity and auspiciousness.

Celebrate the Light

Light the diyas and bring the festive night to life.

May the light you kindle tonight stay with you through the year.
